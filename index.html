About

I am a fixed-term researcher (RTDA) at the Department of Mathematics and Applications “R. Caccioppoli” of the University of Naples Federico II. I received my PhD in Mathematics from the University of Naples Federico II in 2021. Before my current position I was a postdoctoral fellow in Naples and an Alexander von Humboldt postdoctoral fellow at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg.

In 2025 I obtained the Italian National Scientific Qualification (ASN) as Associate Professor in Mathematical Analysis (sector 01/A3).

Research interests

I work on shape optimization and spectral geometry: how the geometry of a domain controls eigenvalues and other quantities attached to elliptic operators, which shapes are optimal, and how stable that optimality is.

  • Shape optimization
  • Spectral geometry
  • Isoperimetric and functional inequalities
  • Quantitative stability estimates
  • Robin and Steklov eigenvalue problems
  • Torsional rigidity
  • Overdetermined problems
  • Hessian and anisotropic operators
  • Symmetrization and comparison results

Research

My research lies in the calculus of variations and the analysis of elliptic PDEs, with a focus on shape optimization, spectral inequalities and their quantitative versions.

Main themes

  • Spectral optimization. Sharp bounds for eigenvalues of the Laplacian and the p-Laplacian with Robin, Steklov and mixed boundary conditions, on convex sets and on domains with holes.
  • Quantitative inequalities. Stability versions of isoperimetric-type inequalities, of the Weinstock inequality and of the Talenti comparison principle.
  • Torsion and rigidity. Torsional rigidity, Serrin-type overdetermined problems and rigidity results, including for Hessian operators and higher order mean curvatures.
  • Anisotropic problems. Finsler and orthotropic operators, anisotropic isoperimetric inequalities and rearrangements of BV functions.
